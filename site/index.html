What else Whistle does

While you're working

Drafts per pane
Each pane keeps its own unsent draft. Switch between agents and every reply waits where you left it.
Quick replies
Keep the phrases you send most, like "Continue", and send one with a tap. Save the key chords you use often, too.
Pictures
Attach a screenshot or photo while composing. The phone shrinks it and drops camera metadata before it goes to your PC.
Review changes
See what an agent changed in its checkout since the last commit, file by file, with the diff.
Terminal history
Swipe back through everything the pane printed, or copy it as plain text. Latest takes you back to the cursor.

Safe by default

Watching comes first
Opening a pane never resizes or focuses your desktop terminal. Typing starts only after you tap Take control, and a reconnect never resends anything.
Your existing sessions
Whistle works with the agents and panes you already run in Herdr. It adds no model calls of its own.
Runs on your computer
Whistle runs on your PC, with no relay or cloud service of its own. Your phone reaches it over a network you already use, such as Tailscale.
Paired phones only
Each phone pairs once by scanning a single-use QR code. You can list and revoke paired devices at any time.

Install with one command

Downloads the right build, checks its checksum, and starts Whistle as a service for your user. If Tailscale is running, Whistle listens on the PC's Tailscale address.

Set it up on your computer

Your phone needs a way to reach your PC

Whistle doesn't provide a network connection. It listens on your PC, and your phone connects to it over a network you set up yourself, for example:

Tailscale
A private network between your own devices. Install it on the PC and the phone, and Whistle listens on the PC's Tailscale address.
Cloudflare Tunnel
Gives the PC an HTTPS address without opening a port. Whistle listens on 127.0.0.1 and the tunnel connects to it; install with --public-origin set to the tunnel's address. The address is on the public internet, so put Cloudflare Access in front of it.

  1. Install Whistle on the PC

    Use the one-line installer or a package from above. The PC needs Linux with systemd, Herdr for your agents, and a network your phone can reach it on.

  2. Start the service

    The one-line installer already did this. After a package install, run this once as your regular user. Whistle starts at login and listens only on your Tailscale address. For Cloudflare Tunnel, change the options as the network note describes.

    whistle install --host "$(tailscale ip -4)" --now

  3. Pair your phone

    Run the pair command on the PC and scan the QR code with your phone's camera. The code works once and expires after five minutes.

    whistle pair

Alerts and installing Whistle as a phone app need HTTPS. The README shows how with Tailscale Serve, along with upgrades and safety notes. If your phone can't connect, whistle doctor on the PC says what's missing.

This website only describes Whistle. It has no login, no terminal connection and no access to your computer. Pair your phone with the Whistle on your own PC.
